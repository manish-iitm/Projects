About

HTML Online Viewer is a free HTML editor, formatter and viewer with an instant live preview. Paste or type markup, and the sandboxed preview updates as you go. Format, search, replace, fold, import and export, with nothing to install.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt 1 Preview in a new tab
  • Alt 2 Highlighted source in a new tab
  • Alt 3 Format
  • Alt 4 Expand all, Alt 5 Collapse all
  • Alt 6 Sample, Alt 7 Clear
  • Alt 8 Import, Alt 9 Export
  • Alt 0 Light/dark mode, Alt A About
  • Ctrl S Save, Alt P Projects, Alt U Publish, Alt C Console
  • Ctrl F Find, Ctrl H Find and replace

Privacy

Everything is processed locally in your browser and kept in local storage so it survives a reload. Code only leaves your device when you choose Save or Publish, and then it goes to your own Puter account.
